# GreenLeaf-Foods-Nigeria-Ltd---HR-Analytics
Dashboard that helps management understand  headcount, attrition, pay, and performance 


## 📊 Project Overview

The GreenLeaf HR Analytics Dashboard is an Excel-based HR analytics project designed to transform employee data into actionable workforce insights.

The project analyzes employee demographics, attrition, compensation, performance, hiring trends, salary bands, and employee tenure to help HR management understand workforce patterns and make data-driven decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project were to:

- Analyze overall employee headcount
- Calculate employee attrition rate
- Analyze attrition by department and location
- Examine salary distribution across departments and grades
- Identify employees whose salaries fall outside their appropriate salary bands
- Analyze employee performance ratings
- Examine hiring trends over time
- Analyze workforce distribution by gender
- Calculate employee tenure
- Build an interactive HR dashboard
- Provide practical HR recommendations based on the analysis

## 🔍 Key Insights

### Attrition

GreenLeaf has 180 employees, with 42 employees having exited the organization, resulting in an overall attrition rate of 23.33%.

Logistics recorded the highest departmental attrition rate, while Customer Service and Sales recorded the highest number of exits.

### Compensation

The overall average monthly salary is NGN 204,617.

There are noticeable differences in average salaries across departments. Operations has the lowest departmental average salary, while Logistics records one of the highest averages.

The analysis also includes salary-band checks to identify employees whose salaries fall outside their appropriate grade ranges.

### Performance

Employee performance is concentrated around ratings 3 and 4.

A total of 80 employees received performance ratings of 4 or 5, representing approximately 44.4% of the workforce.

## 💡 Recommendations

1. Develop targeted employee retention strategies for departments with higher attrition.

2. Review salary structures and grade bands to ensure fair and consistent compensation.

3. Provide training and development opportunities for employees with lower performance ratings.

4. Recognize and create career-development opportunities for high-performing employees.

5. Continuously monitor workforce trends by department and location using HR analytics.
